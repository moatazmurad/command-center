Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Command Center is a personal task manager. This page explains what it does with your information.

What the app accesses

  • Your name and email address from your Google account, used only to greet you and to show who has access to a shared workspace.
  • One file in your Google Drive, called “Command Center data.json”, which the app creates or which you choose. The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.

Where your data is stored

Your tasks and projects are stored only in that file in your own Google Drive, and in your browser on your device so the app works offline. There is no Command Center server, and the app's developer cannot see your data.

Sharing

Your data is shared only with people you choose, through Google Drive sharing. It is never sold, used for advertising, or sent to anyone else.

Removing your data

Sign out in the app's Settings to clear it from your device, and delete “Command Center data.json” from Google Drive to remove it entirely. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

Command Center's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
